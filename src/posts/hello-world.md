Hi, I'm Maj Kravos, a software engineer with four years of experience building full-stack web and mobile products. In my day-to-day professional work, I currently utilize my knowledge of Go, Java, React, AWS, iOS, and Android to deliver solutions for clients in the hospitality industry. I also have a background in DevOps and infrastructure, which helps me understand the full lifecycle of the products I build.

I wanted to start this blog as a way to share my experiences and insights from working in the tech industry, personal projects and experiments, and all other things that interest me in the space. I hope to provide value to other developers by sharing lessons learned, best practices, and interesting projects I've worked on.

## What I work on

Most of my recent work has been in hospitality tech at **Percipia**, where I shipped a few products end to end:

- **[Parallax](https://percipia.com/parallax)** — fully rebuilt the frontend of Percipia's core PMS middleware engine in React, replacing a legacy Java Server Pages interface with a responsive design that works across different device types and screen sizes, and added new features like an In-House Guests dashboard and a new reporting interface.
- **[VIVA+](https://percipia.com/viva+)** — led development of a hospitality-integrated Alexa skill, working with Amazon's Alexa for Hospitality team to provide a multimodal digital compendium for hotel guests, including voice and touch interactions, and integrating with our PMS middleware engine and CMS to provide personalized information and services.
- **[Latitude](https://percipia.com/mobilesolution)** — delivered client-specific builds of a guest-focused mobile kiosk app, with custom UI/UX and third-party integrations including Akia Chat, Schneider Electric EcoStruxure, Knowcross, and CVPS, deployed on Android tablets across many hotel properties.
- **[Frequency Connect](https://percipia.com/connect)** — adapted and white-labeled the open-source Linphone mobile VoIP softphone for use with Percipia's Frequency PBX product, providing a mobile extension for hotel staff to make and receive calls on their work number with features such as call transfer, voicemail, and push notifications, and delivered the application across iOS and Android.

I've also built internal tooling like **ASARConnect**, an AWS-hosted web app for [ASAR Digital](https://asardigital.com/) to onboard and manage their freelancers and offshore staff.

## What I'll write about

This blog is where I'll think out loud about:

- My personal projects, like [Garage](https://github.com/mkravos/Garage) (a vehicle maintenance tracking app for hobbyist mechanics), my professional career, and side projects I am working on.
- My forays into repairing and repurposing old hardware such as my home server (a 2017 Dell XPS 9560 laptop running Linux), self-hosting, and tinkering with cool open source projects.
- How I'm approaching learning new technologies, tools, and frameworks, and sharing resources that I find helpful.
- Thoughts on the tech industry in general.
- Whatever else I want to write about!

## Why bother

Writing helps me to solidify my understanding of the topics I care about, and I'm hoping to compile a repository of information that I can look back on and reference in the future. I also want to share useful information with other developers, and hopefully spark some interesting discussions via email. I don't have a specific schedule in mind for posting, but I plan to write whenever I have something interesting to share or reflect on.

That's the plan. Thanks for reading! If something here resonates, [email me](mailto:contact@majkravos.com).
